Day 1
Find your product
  1. Decide. Commit to seeing it through. No failing, only learning.

Questions

Frequently asked

What if I don't have a product idea yet?

That's exactly what Day 1 is for. You'll list the transformations you've already walked yourself or others through, then narrow it down to the one people most want and will pay for.

Do I need experience creating or selling digital products?

No. You don't need experience creating products, but you do need to have solved the problem yourself. Always start with a problem you've actually solved for yourself or someone else. That's your social proof, and it's what makes your product sell.

Do I need a big following?

Not at all. You'll poll your current audience to validate your idea, even if you don't have many followers. The right people matter more than the number of people.

What tools do I need?

A free Canva account works for most products, plus Stan Store to sell and deliver your product. The program walks you through setting everything up, and it recommends free options wherever possible.

What kind of product will I create?

Whatever best gets your customer their result: a PDF guide, e-book, checklist, template, printables, swipe file, audio recording, video workshop, live challenge, or course. The program helps you choose.

What if I can't finish in one weekend?

That's okay. This isn't a race. Your progress saves automatically, so you can pick up right where you left off. Give yourself the time and space to create something you're proud of.
